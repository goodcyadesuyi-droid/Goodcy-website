Risk comes first

Forex and leveraged products are high risk. You can lose some or all of the money you deposit.

Our learning principles
Practise on demo first. Never trade money you cannot afford to lose. Avoid borrowed money, guaranteed-return claims and pressure to deposit. Understand leverage and position size before placing any trade.
