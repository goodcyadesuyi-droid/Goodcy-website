Install Mamba FX Academy on iPhone

Add the website to your iPhone Home Screen so it opens like an app.

iPhone instructions
  1. Open this website in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share icon.
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. Tap “Add”. The Mamba FX icon will appear on your Home Screen.

This installs the website as a web app. A native iPhone App Store release requires a separate iOS app build and Apple review.
